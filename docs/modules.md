# HaritAyan Platform Modules Dossier

HaritAyan integrates 4 core domains addressing climate resilience and agricultural profitability across Vadodara District:

---

## Module 01: Urban Heat Mitigation & Vadodara Tree Cooling Simulator
- **Objective:** Visualizing surface urban heat islands (SUHI) across Vadodara's administrative wards and simulating the microclimate cooling benefits of strategic urban afforestation.
- **Key Features:**
  - Interactive multi-layer heat index map (Alkapuri, Sayajiganj, Manjalpur, Makarpura, Gotri, Gorwa, Waghodia Road).
  - Tree canopy cooling calculator modeling ambient temperature reduction ($-1.5^\circ\text{C}$ to $-3.5^\circ\text{C}$) based on species leaf area index (Neem, Peepal, Banyan, Gulmohar).
  - Strategic tree plantation recommendations based on road width and thermal exposure.

---

## Module 02: Multimodal Crop Pathology Vision Scanner
- **Objective:** Instant foliar disease diagnosis, pathogen typing, and dual-protocol remediation for Vadodara's cash and staple crops.
- **Key Features:**
  - Multimodal AI powered by Google Gemini 3.8 Flash Vision with Excess Green Index (ExG) leaf validation.
  - Bilingual reporting in clear English and authentic Gujarati.
  - Dual Treatment Standard:
    1. *Biological / Organic:* Neem oil, Trichoderma viride, Jeevamrut formulations.
    2. *Chemical Precision:* ICAR / AAU approved active ingredients (e.g. Flonicamid 50 WG, Mancozeb 75 WP) with exact pump dosages.
  - Interactive sample demo query shelf with 1-click verification.

---

## Module 03: Real-Time APMC Mandi Market Prices Engine
- **Objective:** Providing daily commodity auction prices, arrivals, and price arbitrage across key Vadodara district market yards.
- **Key Features:**
  - 5 District APMC Mandis: **Sayajiganj**, **Padra**, **Karjan**, **Dabhoi**, **Savli**.
  - Key Crops: Cotton (કપાસ), Castor (દિવેલા), Pigeon Pea (તુવેર), Tomato (ટામેટા), Okra (ભીંડા), Wheat (ઘઉં).
  - 1-Click Gujarat Unit Toggle: Metric **₹/Quintal (100 kg)** $\leftrightarrow$ Traditional **₹/Mann (૨૦ કિલો મણ)**.
  - 7-Day Sparkline Trend Charts & Daily Arrival volumes.
  - Cross-Mandi Arbitrage Matrix highlighting where selling produce yields highest profit.
  - District APMC Directory with operational hours and superintendent contacts.

---

## Module 04: Operations, Weather Alerts & Agronomist Review Station
- **Objective:** Empowering District Agricultural Officers and farmers with early warning heat alerts and structured consultation workflows.
- **Key Features:**
  - District heatwave early warnings and seasonal agro-advisories.
  - Farmer Consultation Desk: Farmers submit unreviewed field issues with photos.
  - Officer Review Station: Extension officers review, diagnose, and sign official treatment prescriptions.
