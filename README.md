# HaritAyan

HaritAyan is an AI-powered smart agriculture and plant management platform developed to assist farmers, gardeners, and researchers in monitoring plant health and improving crop productivity. The system uses computer vision, machine learning, and data analysis techniques to identify plant species, detect diseases, analyze environmental conditions, and provide personalized recommendations.

By allowing users to upload images of plants, HaritAyan can recognize visible symptoms of diseases and suggest appropriate preventive measures. The platform also offers guidance regarding irrigation, soil quality, fertilizer usage, pest control, and weather-based decision-making. Through continuous learning and data-driven insights, the system helps users make informed decisions while minimizing resource wastage.

The primary objective of HaritAyan is to promote sustainable agricultural practices, increase efficiency, reduce crop losses, and make modern agricultural technology more accessible to everyone. The integration of artificial intelligence with an intuitive user interface enables users to monitor and manage plant growth effectively and efficiently.
